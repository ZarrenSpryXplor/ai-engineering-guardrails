# Code of conduct

We want a constructive, technically rigorous community. Be respectful, assume good intent, discuss ideas rather than people, and make room for contributors with different backgrounds and experience levels.

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, threats, doxxing, deliberate intimidation, or the publication of another person's private information are not acceptable in project spaces.

If you experience or witness a conduct concern, do not create a public issue with sensitive details. Contact the maintainer at [zarren.spry@xplortechnologies.com](mailto:zarren.spry@xplortechnologies.com). Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical, with appropriate action determined by the maintainer.

This document does not replace GitHub's own community guidelines or reporting mechanisms.
